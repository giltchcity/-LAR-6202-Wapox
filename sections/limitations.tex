\section{Limitations}
\label{sec:limitations}

WarpVox assumes a static scene, valid geometry, and a locally coherent pre-correction TSDF. It cannot create surface absent from the source map or repair incorrectly fused topology, and repeated or nearly overlapping surfaces may still yield a consistent but less correct association. The keyframe cache grows with keyframes. Finite-resolution TSDF reconstruction cannot exactly reproduce the reintegrated map. Small number of small spurious faces may be auto-removed. While the attainable accuracy remains limited by residual error in the corrected trajectory, severe failures require reintegration from the original measurements.
