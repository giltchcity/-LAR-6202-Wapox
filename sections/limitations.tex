\section{Limitations}
\label{sec:limitations}

WarpVox assumes a static scene, valid geometry, and a locally coherent pre-correction TSDF. It cannot create surface absent from the source map or repair incorrectly fused topology, and repeated or nearly overlapping surfaces may still yield a consistent but less correct association. The keyframe cache grows with keyframes. Finite-resolution TSDF reconstruction cannot exactly reproduce the reintegrated map. The bounded-distortion guard may leave holes where no valid deformation is found, while the attainable accuracy remains limited by residual error in the corrected trajectory. Severe failures require reintegration from the original measurements.
