\section{Limitations}
\label{sec:limitations}
WarpVox targets static scenes and corrects the geometry already in the map:
regions first observed after a correction enter through continued fusion, and
a severely failed trajectory can still be restored by reintegration from the
original measurements, since the output remains a native Voxblox TSDF. The
keyframe cache grows with session length, at 2.1\,GiB on average for the
RGB-D sequences (Sec.~\ref{sec:experiments}).
