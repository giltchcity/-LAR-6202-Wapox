\section{Limitations}
\label{sec:limitations}

 WarpVox assumes a static scene, valid calibration, and a locally coherent pre-correction TSDF. It cannot recover missing geometry or repair corrupted topology, while repeated or overlapping surfaces may yield incorrect associations. The keyframe cache grows with retained keyframes, and finite-resolution TSDF reconstruction cannot exactly reproduce the reintegrated map. The attainable accuracy also remains limited by residual error in the corrected trajectory. Severe failures require reintegration from the original measurements.
