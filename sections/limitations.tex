\section{Limitations}
\label{sec:limitations}
WarpVox targets static scenes and corrects the geometry already in the map:
later observations enter through continued fusion, and severe trajectory
failures remain recoverable by reintegration from the original measurements.
The keyframe cache grows with session length, at 2.1\,GiB on average for
RGB-D. Beyond the large-scale benchmarks evaluated
here, future work covers dynamic scenes, cache compaction, and longer
sessions with more loop closures.
