\section{Method}
\label{sec:method}

WarpVox corrects an existing truncated signed distance field (TSDF) after
loop closure, using stored keyframe measurements and the externally
corrected trajectory. TSDF fusion forms each surface point from the range
measurements that observed it, placed by their frame poses; a loop
closure revises those poses but leaves the measurements valid in their
sensor frames. WarpVox traces the fusion backwards: Stage~1 recovers, for
each surface vertex, the keyframe measurements that formed it; Stage~2
places them under the corrected poses as observation-derived targets; Stage~3 propagates these targets over the surface; and Stage~4
combines the corrected geometry with source support, sign, weights, and
free space into a native TSDF for continued fusion.

\subsection{Problem and Correction Inputs}
\label{sec:problem}

Following Voxgraph~\cite{reijgwart2020voxgraph}, the pre-correction TSDF is
assumed locally valid: surface geometry and connectivity stay coherent in
local neighbourhoods despite global inconsistency, and severely noisy or
topologically corrupted source maps are outside our scope.

For $x\in\mathbb R^3$, $\widetilde x=[x^\top,1]^\top$ denotes homogeneous
coordinates. $T^-(t),T^+(t)\in SE(3)$ map the sensor frame at time $t$ to the
world frame before and after the correction.
The map built with $T^-$ is a Voxblox TSDF~\cite{Oleynikova_2017}: a regular
grid of voxel size $s$, allocated sparsely in blocks. Each voxel centre $x$
stores a truncated signed distance $\Phi^-(x)\in[-\tau,\tau]$, positive on the
sensor side, and an integration weight $w^-(x)\ge0$, zero if unallocated. A
voxel is \emph{supported} if its weight is positive; the supported voxels
form the observed support $\Omega^-$.
At a correction, the Marching-Cubes surface~\cite{10.1145/37402.37422} that
Voxblox maintains during fusion becomes the source surface
$\mathcal M^-=(V^-,F)$: vertex positions $V^-=\{v_j^-\}$, triangle indices
$F$, and area-weighted vertex normals $n_j^-$.
WarpVox stores one timestamped range image per keyframe (KF) in its sensor
frame, $\mathcal C_{\rm KF}=\{(t_i,D_i)\}$: the depth image of each SLAM
keyframe (RGB-D) or the spherical range image of each posed scan (LiDAR).
Fig.~\ref{fig:pipeline} summarizes the four stages,
Table~\ref{tab:params} lists all parameters, and
Figs.~\ref{fig:mech_assoc} -- \ref{fig:mech_recon} illustrate association,
clustering, and reconstructed support.

Stage~1 recovers the measurements that formed the surface, associating
source vertices with geometrically consistent keyframe measurements under
$T^-$, the poses used to build the map. For each queried vertex $v_j^-$, a
$k$-d tree (RGB-D) or grid (LiDAR) over the sensor centres retrieves
candidate keyframes within the index radius. For candidate $r$, with
timestamp $t_r$ and range image $D_r$, the vertex is mapped into its sensor
frame and sampled:
\begin{equation}
\hat v_{rj}^-=\bigl[[T^-(t_r)]^{-1}\widetilde v_j^-\bigr]_{1:3},\qquad
m_{rj}=\mathcal S_m\bigl(D_r,\Pi_m(\hat v_{rj}^-)\bigr).
\label{eq:sensor_sample}
\end{equation}
For modality $m$, $\Pi_m$ is the pinhole (RGB-D) or spherical (LiDAR)
projection, and $\mathcal S_m$ returns one sensor-frame point
$m_{rj}\in\mathbb R^3$, or none: the depth bilinearly interpolated from at
least two valid pixels and back-projected \mbox{(RGB-D)}, or the
return closest to $\hat v_{rj}^-$ in a $3{\times}3$ window (LiDAR).

Let $z_{rj}^-$ and $z_{rj}^m$ be the depth (RGB-D) or range (LiDAR) of
$\hat v_{rj}^-$ and $m_{rj}$, $\delta_{rj}=\|m_{rj}-\hat v_{rj}^-\|$, and
$\phi_{rj}$ the angle between $n_j^-$ and the viewing ray. A sample must
pass the gate $\delta_{rj}\le g_0+g_1z_{rj}^-$, which for LiDAR also bounds
$|z_{rj}^m-z_{rj}^-|$, and receives the reliability
\begin{equation}
\alpha_{rj}=\frac{|\cos\phi_{rj}|}{1+z_{rj}^-/z_0}\,
e^{-\delta_{rj}/\sigma_g}\,e^{-|z_{rj}^m-z_{rj}^-|/\sigma_z},
\label{eq:alpha}
\end{equation}
with range scale $z_0$ and discrepancy scales $\sigma_g,\sigma_z$; the last
factor applies to RGB-D only. A candidate is retained if its sample passes
the gate and, for RGB-D, $\alpha_{rj}\ge\alpha_{\min}$. Each vertex keeps the
at most $n_{\max}$ retained candidates of largest $\alpha_{rj}$ as its
\emph{observations} $(t_r,m_{rj},\alpha_{rj})\in\mathcal O_j$. A vertex with
$\mathcal O_j\neq\emptyset$ is \emph{observed}; unobserved vertices follow
the Stage-3 deformation without a measured target.

\subsection{Stage 2: Corrected Surface Targets}
\label{sec:stage2}

The matched measurements keep their sensor-frame coordinates when the
trajectory changes. Stage~2 places them with the corrected keyframe pose
$\widehat T_r^+$, retrieved by timestamp $t_r$ (RGB-D, interpolated if
needed) or scan index (LiDAR), giving the \emph{corrected sample}
\begin{equation}
q_{rj}^+=\bigl[\widehat T_r^+\widetilde m_{rj}\bigr]_{1:3}.
\label{eq:corrected_target}
\end{equation}
Let $\mathcal J_j$ index the observations of $v_j^-$ whose pose exists; a
vertex with $\mathcal J_j=\emptyset$ is treated as unobserved. Each sample is
weighted by its reliability, damped with scale $\zeta_m$ by depth (RGB-D),
$\omega_{rj}=\alpha_{rj}/(1+z_{rj}^m/\zeta_m)$, or by displacement (LiDAR),
$\omega_{rj}=\alpha_{rj}/(1+\|q_{rj}^+-v_j^-\|/\zeta_m)$. Samples farther than
$\theta_j=\max(\epsilon_m,\kappa\operatorname{median}_{r\in\mathcal J_j}\|q_{rj}^+-\bar q_j\|)$
from their $\omega$-weighted mean $\bar q_j$ are dropped, leaving $I_j$;
$\epsilon_m$ is a floor at the modality's sampling resolution,
$\kappa>1$ keeps $I_j$ nonempty, and with fewer than three samples none is
dropped. The observation-derived target is
\begin{equation}
v_j^*=\frac{\sum_{r\in I_j}\omega_{rj}q_{rj}^+}{\sum_{r\in I_j}\omega_{rj}},
\label{eq:target}
\end{equation}
and each observed vertex has displacement $d_j=v_j^*-v_j^-$.

\subsection{Stage 3: Surface Deformation}
\label{sec:stage3}

Stage~3 propagates the targets over the surface by as-rigid-as-possible
(ARAP) deformation~\cite{ARAP_modeling:2007}, which penalizes local shape
distortion while fixing \emph{controls} at prescribed positions; observed
vertices give \emph{measured controls}. Large meshes are solved on a
component-aware proxy whose motion is transferred back, so the source and
warped meshes share their triangles. Connected components follow mesh edges
and are relabelled at each correction; \emph{fragments} have fewer than
$N_{\rm f}$ faces. Algorithm~\ref{alg:stage3} orders steps (a) -- (f).

\begin{algorithm}[t]
\caption{Stage~3 of one correction}
\label{alg:stage3}
\footnotesize
\begin{algorithmic}[1]
\Require source mesh $(V^-,F)$; measured controls $d_j$
\Ensure paired surfaces $\bar{\mathcal M}^-,\mathcal M^+$
\State label components; remove degenerate faces and control-free fragments
\State (a) remove outlier controls once
\State (b) omit fragments without controls; add synthetic controls
\If{$|V^-|>1.3P_{\max}$}
  \State (c) build proxy $\mathcal P^-$; (d) proxy targets, Eq.~\eqref{eq:welsch}
  \State (e) solve Eq.~\eqref{eq:arap} on $\mathcal P^-$; (f) transfer to source
\Else
  \State (e) solve Eq.~\eqref{eq:arap} on the source mesh
\EndIf
\For{at most $n_{\rm rep}$ passes} \Comment{(f)}
  \State relax invalid faces; stop if none or no progress
\EndFor
\State remove faces violating $\tau_{\rm e}$ or $\tau_{\rm A}$ and omitted components
\end{algorithmic}
\end{algorithm}

\textit{(a) Outlier controls.}
The prediction $\hat d_j$ averages the displacements of the $k$ nearest other
controls with weights $\|v_j^--v_i^-\|^{-2}$, using only same-component
neighbours when at least $n_{\min}$ exist, so that nearby surface copies
caused by drift keep separate motions. With $e_j=\|d_j-\hat d_j\|$,
$\mu=\operatorname{median}_je_j$, and the median absolute deviation
$\mathrm{MAD}=\operatorname{median}_j|e_j-\mu|$~\cite{rousseeuw1993mad},
control $j$ is an \emph{outlier} if
$e_j>\mu+\gamma\max(1.4826\,\mathrm{MAD},\sigma_{\min})$.

\textit{(b) Components without controls.}
A fragment that lost all its controls is \emph{omitted}; every other
component without a control receives three \emph{synthetic controls} at
mutually distant vertices, with the coordinate-wise median displacement of
the $k$ controls nearest to its centroid.

\textit{(c) Proxy.}
A mesh with more than $1.3P_{\max}$ vertices is deformed through a proxy
$\mathcal P^-=(P^-,F_P)$ built by vertex
clustering~\cite{rossignac1993vertexclustering}: each (cell, component) pair
of cubic cells with side $h=(A_{\mathcal M}/P_{\max})^{1/2}$, $A_{\mathcal M}$
the mesh area, becomes one proxy vertex $p_a^-$, placed with the quadric
error metric (QEM)~\cite{garland1997qem}; $\pi(j)$ is the proxy vertex of
$v_j^-$.

\textit{(d) Proxy targets.}
Vertex $a$ starts from
$p_a^{(0)}=p_a^-+|\mathcal K_a|^{-1}\sum_{j\in\mathcal K_a}d_j$, the mean
displacement of its controls $\mathcal K_a=\{j\in\mathcal C:\pi(j)=a\}$
among the surviving measured and synthetic controls $\mathcal C$, and is
\emph{controlled} if $\mathcal K_a\neq\emptyset$; a proxy component without
one has three vertices controlled at $p_a^{(0)}=p_a^-$. $\mathcal M_P$ holds
the controlled vertices with a measured control; the others have
\emph{synthetic} targets. A local rigid fit $(\hat R_a,\hat t_a)$ to the pairs
$(p_b^-,p_b^{(0)})$ of the $k$ nearest $b\in\mathcal M_P\setminus\{a\}$, by
Procrustes alignment~\cite{schoenemann1966procrustes} reweighted with the
Welsch weights $\nu(r)=e^{-r^2/2\sigma_a^2}$~\cite{yao2020welsch} of its
residuals at robust scale $\sigma_a$, predicts
$\hat p_a=\hat R_ap_a^-+\hat t_a$. A measured target is \emph{gross} if
$r_a=\|p_a^{(0)}-\hat p_a\|\ge c_{\rm g}\sigma_a$, and $2G_K>N_K$ denotes a
strict majority of gross targets among the $N_K$ fitted measured targets of
component $K$. The constrained target is
\begin{equation}
p_a^*=\begin{cases}
\hat p_a, & a\ \text{gross},\ 2G_K>N_K,\\
\hat p_a+\nu(r_a)(p_a^{(0)}-\hat p_a), & a\notin\mathcal M_P,\\
p_a^{(0)}, & \text{otherwise},
\end{cases}
\label{eq:welsch}
\end{equation}
and a vertex with too few neighbours for a fit keeps $p_a^{(0)}$.

\textit{(e) ARAP.}
The deformed proxy positions $P^+=\{p_a^+\}$ are fixed to $p_a^*$ at
controlled vertices $\mathcal C_P$ and optimized elsewhere, with one rotation
$R_a\in SO(3)$ per vertex:
\begin{equation}
\min_{P^+,\{R_a\}}\sum_{a}\!\sum_{f\in\operatorname{star}_P(a)}
\sum_{(b,c)\in f}\!\tfrac{c^f_{bc}}{3}
\|(p_b^+-p_c^+)-R_a(p_b^--p_c^-)\|^2,
\label{eq:arap}
\end{equation}
where $\operatorname{star}_P(a)$ is the one-ring of proxy triangles incident
to $a$, $(b,c)$ runs over their edges, and
$c^f_{bc}=\tfrac12\cot\vartheta^f_{bc}$ uses the angle opposite $(b,c)$ in
rest triangle $f$; summing over incident faces gives the symmetric
cotangent weight $c_{bc}=\sum_{f\supset(b,c)}c^f_{bc}$~\cite{10.1007/978-3-662-05105-4_2}.
Libigl~\cite{libigl} runs 20 local -- global steps.

\textit{(f) Transfer and repair.}
Each source vertex takes the proxy motion \emph{topology-locally}, from the
rest triangle $f_j$ of $\operatorname{star}_P(\pi(j))$ closest to $v_j^-$:
$v_j^+=v_j^-+\sum_{b\in f_j}\lambda_{jb}(p_b^+-p_b^-)$, with $\lambda_{jb}$
the barycentric coordinates of the closest point, so the transfer stays
within its component. Repair flags a face as \emph{invalid} if its edge
stretch $\xi^{\rm e}=\max_e\ell^+_e/\ell^-_e\ge\tau_{\rm e}$, area growth
$\xi^{\rm A}=A^+/A^-\ge\tau_{\rm A}$, or aspect ratio
$\xi^{\rm asp}=(\ell^+_{\max})^2/(2A^+)\ge\tau_{\rm asp}$ with
$\ell^+_{\max}\ge\ell_{\min}$, for source and warped edge lengths $\ell^\pm$
and areas $A^\pm$; Jacobi relaxation smooths the displacements of its
vertices with all others fixed. The retained triangles $\bar F\subseteq F$
define the paired surfaces $\bar{\mathcal M}^-=(\bar V^-,\bar F)$ and
$\mathcal M^+=(\bar V^+,\bar F)$.

\subsection{Stage 4: Source-Supported TSDF Reconstruction}
\label{sec:stage4}

\begin{figure}[t]
  \centering
  \includegraphics[width=0.764\columnwidth]{Figures/mech_recon.pdf}
  \caption{Stage~4: transferred, fallback, and free-space support.}
  \label{fig:mech_recon}
\end{figure}

Stage~4 restores the volumetric state for continued fusion at the source
resolution: $\mathcal M^+$ provides the zero set and distance magnitudes, and
the source TSDF $(\Phi^-,w^-)$ supplies observed support and integration
weights and guides the sign.

\emph{Surface-local reconstruction} covers voxel centres $x$ within
$\tau$ of $\mathcal M^+$ in blocks with a warped vertex and their 26
neighbours. A closest-triangle query~\cite{10.1145/2601097.2601199} returns face $f=(a,b,c)$, closest point $q^+$, and barycentric
coordinates $\beta$, and $q^-=\sum_{k\in f}\beta_k\bar v_k^-$ on $\bar{\mathcal M}^-$. With unit normals $n_f^\pm$ of $f$ on $\bar{\mathcal M}^-$ and
$\mathcal M^+$, oriented by the vertex order of $F$, and unit directions
$t_f^\pm$ of edge $(a,b)$, frames
$R_f^\pm=[t_f^\pm,\,n_f^\pm\times t_f^\pm,\,n_f^\pm]\in SO(3)$ define the
local-rigid pullback
\begin{equation}
\Psi_f(x)=q^-+R_f^-(R_f^+)^\top(x-q^+),
\label{eq:inverse_map}
\end{equation}
which maps the surface-relative offset into the source triangle's
frame~\cite{sumner2004deformationtransfer}; let $x^-=\Psi_f(x)$,
$r(x)=\|x-q^+\|$, and $d^+(x)=\min(\tau,r(x))$. A trilinear source sample at
$x^-$ is \emph{valid} if every source voxel with a nonzero coefficient is
supported, and \emph{sign-consistent} if their distances share one
nonzero sign. The transferred weight, capped at $w_{\max}$, is
\begin{equation}
w_{\rm src}^+(x)=\begin{cases}
\bar w^-(x^-), & \text{valid sample at } x^-,\\
w^-(y_0(x^-)), & \text{otherwise},
\end{cases}
\label{eq:source_weight}
\end{equation}
with interpolated weight $\bar w^-$ and containing voxel $y_0(x^-)$;
$x$ is \emph{source-supported} if $w_{\rm src}^+(x)>0$. With geometric sign
$s_f^+(x)=\operatorname{sgn}((x-q^+)^\top n_f^+)$, the source TSDF sets the
sign
\begin{equation}
s_{\rm tr}(x)=\begin{cases}
s_f^+(x)\operatorname{sgn}(g_f), & g_f\ \text{available},\\
\operatorname{sgn}\bar\Phi^-(x^-), & \text{else, if sign-consistent},\\
s_f^+(x), & \text{otherwise},
\end{cases}
\label{eq:sign}
\end{equation}
where $\bar\Phi^-$ is the interpolated source distance, and
$g_f=[\bar\Phi^-(q^-+s\,n_f^-)-\bar\Phi^-(q^--s\,n_f^-)]/(2s)$, its central
difference across the paired source face, calibrates the normal toward free
space, available if all three samples are valid and $g_f\neq0$.
\emph{Transferred} voxels are source-supported: narrow set
$\mathcal N_{\rm tr}$ with $r(x)\le\rho$, and extended set $\mathcal E$
with $\rho<r(x)\le\tau$, which keeps a voxel with a valid sample only if
$g_f$ is available.

\emph{Fallback} supplies a surface prior where the pullback has no source
support, $w_{\rm src}^+(x)=0$ and $r(x)\le\rho$, with weight
$w_{\rm fb}(x)=\operatorname{clamp}_{[0,1]}((\rho-r(x))/s)$, withheld where
$q^+$ lies on a boundary edge (incident to one face) or its endpoint; the
voxels with $w_{\rm fb}(x)>0$ form $\Omega_{\rm fb}$. After removing the
voxels of $\mathcal E$ that would add a zero crossing or are disconnected from
$\mathcal N_{\rm tr}\cup\Omega_{\rm fb}$, $\mathcal N_{\rm tr}$ and the rest of
$\mathcal E$ form $\Omega_{\rm tr}$.

\emph{Observed-volume transport} carries the source state through the same
paired frames. Observed source voxels $y\in\Omega^-$ are grouped into
truncation-sized cells; each cell $c$ uses the source face closest to its
observed-voxel centroid, unless it or its warped copy is degenerate, and the
paired warped face to define $\Gamma_c(y)=q_c^++R_c^+(R_c^-)^\top(y-q_c^-)$,
and output voxels are sampled at $\Gamma_c^{-1}(x)$. On the reconstructed
band, transported contributions not represented by its pullback add the
weight $w_{\rm add}^+(x)$; elsewhere they fill empty voxels without new zero
crossings, forming $\Omega_{\rm vol}$ with $\Phi_{\rm vol}^+$ and
$w_{\rm vol}^+$.

The three sets are disjoint, and
$\Omega^+=\Omega_{\rm tr}\cup\Omega_{\rm fb}\cup\Omega_{\rm vol}$ is the set of
supported output voxels:
\begin{equation}
(\Phi^+,\widetilde w^+)=\begin{cases}
(s_{\rm tr}d^+,\,w_{\rm src}^++w_{\rm add}^+), & x\in\Omega_{\rm tr},\\
(s_f^+d^+,\,w_{\rm fb}+w_{\rm add}^+), & x\in\Omega_{\rm fb},\\
(\Phi_{\rm vol}^+,\,w_{\rm vol}^+), & x\in\Omega_{\rm vol},
\end{cases}
\label{eq:hybrid_tsdf}
\end{equation}
with $w^+=\min(w_{\max},\widetilde w^+)$. The resulting native Voxblox TSDF
replaces the active map, and fusion continues on it.

\subsection{Complexity}
\label{sec:complexity}

Let $V_q$ be the number of queried vertices, $Q$ that of examined
vertex -- keyframe pairs, $K_{\rm KF}$ that of stored keyframes with $|D|$
samples each, $A$ that of Marching-Cubes cells that emit triangles of
$\mathcal M^-$, $P$ that of vertices in the deformation solve (the proxy size,
or $|V^-|$ on the direct path), and $X^-,X^+$ the source and output voxel
slots. A correction costs
$C_{\rm corr}=C_{\rm assoc}+C_{\rm def}(A,P)+C_{\rm vol}(X^-,X^+)$ over
Stages~1 -- 2, 3, and 4. Reading every keyframe and rebuilding the index gives
$C_{\rm assoc}=O(K_{\rm KF}|D|+(K_{\rm KF}+V_q)\log K_{\rm KF}+Q)$ for RGB-D
and $O(K_{\rm KF}|D|+V_q+Q)$ for LiDAR; $C_{\rm def}$ covers proxy
construction, target consolidation, ARAP, transfer, and repair, and
$C_{\rm vol}$ surface-local reconstruction and observed-volume transport. Reintegrating $N$ frames costs $\Theta(N|D|)$.
