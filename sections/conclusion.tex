\section{Conclusion}
WarpVox corrects an active TSDF after loop closure without replaying the
complete frame history. Across RGB-D and LiDAR benchmarks, it remains close
to full reintegration with a $48\times$ mean speedup on RGB-D, and it corrects
the surface more accurately than Kimera-PGMO in less than half the time. The
resulting native Voxblox volume resumes fusion after a seconds-scale update
and supports later corrections, and the same targets also correct a Gaussian
map. Code and configurations will be released.
