\section{Conclusion}
WarpVox corrects an active TSDF after loop closure without replaying the
complete frame history. Across RGB-D and LiDAR benchmarks, it remains close
to full reintegration and reaches a $48\times$ mean speedup on RGB-D. The
resulting native Voxblox volume resumes fusion after a seconds-scale update,
supports later corrections, and retains only a keyframe range cache for
historical measurements. The Gaussian case study suggests that
observation-derived targets may extend beyond voxel grids; code and
configurations will be released.
