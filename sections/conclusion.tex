\section{Conclusion}
WarpVox converts an external trajectory revision into a native Voxblox TSDF without replaying the complete measurement history. Observation-derived targets drive one deformation per correction, after which source-derived support, sign, integration weights, and free space are transferred around the corrected zero set. Across RGB-D and LiDAR benchmarks, WarpVox remains close to full reintegration with mean core-time speedups of $48.43\times$ and $3.23\times$, respectively. In a same-source comparison over ten Kimera-Multi bags, it improves F@25/F@50 over Kimera-PGMO by $0.26/0.91$~pp while reducing corrected-surface time by $2.34\times$. The single-sequence Gaussian-centre case study suggests that the observation-derived targets may transfer beyond voxel grids. The returned native TSDF supports continued fusion and subsequent loop-closure corrections. Code and all configuration files will be released.
