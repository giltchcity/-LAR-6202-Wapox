\section{Conclusion}
WarpVox demonstrates that an active TSDF can be corrected after loop closure
without replaying the complete measurement history. Across RGB-D and LiDAR
benchmarks, it remains close to full reintegration; on RGB-D sequences it
achieves a $48\times$ mean update-time speedup. The corrected output is a
native Voxblox volume that resumes fusion after a seconds-scale update and
supports later corrections; its historical measurement storage is limited to
a keyframe range cache, while full replay requires access to the complete
frame history. The Gaussian case study suggests that observation-derived
targets may extend beyond voxel grids; code and configurations will be
released.
